How the fit score works
  • Each metric is scaled to 0–100 across all models that report it. Indices are scaled linearly; price, cost per task, speed and latency span orders of magnitude and are scaled on a log basis.
  • Fit score = weighted average of the scaled metrics. With "only complete data" off, missing metrics are skipped and remaining weights re-normalized (marked "partial").
  • Monthly cost here uses this use case's own sized tokens (section 2), not a generic workload.
